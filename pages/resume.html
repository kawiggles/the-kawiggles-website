Work History

Aspen View Homes

July 2020 - August 2021

I got this job in 2020 as a way of filling the time between my graduation and when I planned on attending college. The prospect of doing my first year of higher education during the restrictions of the coronavirus was not particularly appealing to me, and so I found a job as a field technician for a local homebuilder. At first, "field technician" just meant someone who cleaned up job sites. However, the position allowed for me to become intimately familiar with the actual labor and management that goes into homebuilding. It is a complicated process with lots of moving pieces that all have to be coordinated in order to work. I spent my time shadowing the site's supervisor, who's responsibility it is to ensure that trade workers and inspections get done on time and in the right order. After a month of working, I was aware enough of the homebuilding process to start anticipating my supervisor's intstructions and completing tasks ahead of schedule. Because of this, my supervisor afforded me more responsibility and freedom, allowing me to set my own schedule and perform tasks as I saw fit. These duties would of course involve cleaning, but would also involve walking houses fixing errors found by customers, preparing houses for various inspections, and managing site inventory. While not a glamourous job, by the end of my time in this position I was well acquainted with the responsibilities of each trade involved in homebuilding, the overall homebuilding pipeline, and the necessary labor skills required to perform each of those trade positions.

Goodwin Knight, LLC

January 2024 - January 2025

Following a couple of difficult academic semesters, I returned home for a year to reorient myself. Because of my prior experience and connections I was able to obtain a job working for a commerical construction firm. Because of the small size of the development team, I was given significantly more responsibility than I had at my previous position. In this job, instead of just learning about the construction process, I was actively involved in coordinating and managing the jobsite. This included using construction/project management tools like BuilderTrend to develop gantt charts and ensure work was properly done according to a tight deadline. It involved documenting damage to preconstructed modules and ordering the necessary materials to replace them. I organized plans for setting modular units, scheduled and participated in building inspections, tracked jobsite expenditures, and worked late to resolve issues as they arose. This role gave me the opportunity to practice real organizational and leadership skills in an environment that was fast paced and dynamic, and I thouroughly enjoyed it. The end result was my project completing on time, despite a number of engineering challenges. While I became proficient at a number of skills specific to construction, the real skills I gained were broader, pertaining to organization and leadership.

Air Force Academy Foundation and Association of Graduates

March 2026 - Present

In my final semester of college, I decided that my interest in computers was great enough to warrant pursing the field as a career. Accordingly, I spent my final months in college and some months afterwards in the pursuit of knowledge and skills related to computers. It is during this time that I configured my Linux network environment, learned how to program in C and C++, began studying for my CompTia exams, and began searching for roles in the technology industry. I was afforded the opportunity to intern at the Air Force Academy Foundation and Association of Graduates through some personal connections. The internship has allowed me to further immerse myself in the world of computing by allowing me to practice applying my knowledge in a true production environment. This is important, as it is difficult to learn and implement skills related to security, reliability, and understanding legacy technology when working solely with a system on which only you are individually reliant on.
